灯会前夕Lantern Eve

A river town, a railing broken on the lantern route, and rumours you can trace back one person at a time. 水乡小镇上，游灯要过的石桥塌了一段栏杆。
镇上每句闲话是经谁传来的，都查得到。

I saw it myself: Li Er was at the bridge last night. 我亲眼看见，
李二昨晚在桥边。
Zhou's Clerk told me that Li Er was at the bridge last night. 周家伙计跟我说，
李二昨晚在桥边。
Zhou Erlang told me that Li Er broke the bridge railing last night. 周家二郎跟我说，
李二昨晚把栏杆弄坏了。
